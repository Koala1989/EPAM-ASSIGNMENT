## Questions for the architect

1. **XF inventory status.** `Cross_Functional_Scenarios/requirements-inventory.md` is marked "pending architect review". None of these cases can be approved until R-XF-01 to R-XF-07 are reviewed.
2. **UI-XF-034, steps 1 and 2.** These steps state as fact that the *Used In* tab and the *BOM* tab are visible when the attribute is on. Q-XF-06 asks, in general, whether each tab is always visible when its attribute is on, and the rules only say "visible only if". Should steps 1 and 2 be marked with Q-XF-06 too, or do you read "visible only if" as also meaning "visible when"?
3. **UI-XF-037 and UI-XF-040.**
   - Both run as `admin`. The settings change is only a pre-step, and Q-Categories-03 records that `engineer` sees the *Part actions* menu with Delete. Is `engineer` (or `allaccess`) the least-privileged user that can delete a part? The docs do not say.
   - Both set the part inactive and then look for it in the category part list (037 step 2, 040 step 2). The docs do not say whether the list shows inactive parts by default. Should this be an open question, or should the steps check the part some other way (for example by its URL)?
4. **UI-XF-011.** This case largely repeats UI-Part_Params-039. Its only cross-Area addition is the parent list membership check, in step 3. Is that enough for it to stay a separate case in XF?
