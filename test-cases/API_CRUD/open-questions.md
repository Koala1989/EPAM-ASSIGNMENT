| Q-CRUD-03 | 2026-10-13 | Part and category CRUD | Open | R-CRUD-10 | [openapi-parts.yaml#L1406](../../requirements/openapi-parts.yaml#L1406) | What happens to the parts and the child categories of a category deleted with `DELETE /api/part/category/{id}/`: are they deleted, moved to the parent, or is the delete refused (and with which status)? | API-REL-001, API-REL-002 | Answered | Observed 2026-10-13 on 1.5.6: the body must contain both `delete_parts` and `delete_child_categories`; without them `400` `{"delete_child_categories": ["This field is required."], "delete_parts": ["This field is required."]}` and nothing is deleted. Both `false`: `204`, the category is deleted and its parts and child categories move up to its parent (a top-level category: parts get `category` `null`, children `parent` `null`). `delete_parts` `true`: its own parts are deleted, children move up with their parts. `delete_child_categories` `true`: children are deleted and their parts get `category` `null`. Both `true`: all deleted. |
| Q-CRUD-04 | 2026-10-13 | Part and category CRUD | Open | R-CRUD-17 | [openapi-parts.yaml#L778](../../requirements/openapi-parts.yaml#L778), [openapi-parts.yaml#L983](../../requirements/openapi-parts.yaml#L983) | How does a bulk `PUT`/`PATCH` on `/api/part/` or `/api/part/category/` select the records to update? A body without `items` returns `400` `{"non_field_errors": "List of items must be provided for bulk operation"}`; `filters` is ignored. `?all=true` was not tried: it updates every record. |
| Q-CRUD-05 | 2026-10-13 | Part and category CRUD | Open | R-CRUD-16 | [openapi-parts.yaml#L4152](../../requirements/openapi-parts.yaml#L4152) | What is the default `PART_NAME_FORMAT` and so the exact `full_name` of a part with a given name, IPN and revision? | API-CRUD-007 | Answered | Observed 2026-10-13 on 1.5.6, matching the default in the app: `{{ part.IPN if part.IPN }}{{ ' \| ' if part.IPN }}{{ part.name }}{{ ' \| ' if part.revision }}{{ part.revision if part.revision }}`. IPN and name give `<IPN> \| <name>`; with revision B `<IPN> \| <name> \| B`; name only gives `<name>`. `\|` stands for a literal `|`. |
